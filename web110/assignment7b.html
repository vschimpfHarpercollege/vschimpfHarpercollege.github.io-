Database Questions and Answers

  1. What fields are contained in the Categories table?
    • CategoryID
    • CategoryName
    • Description
  2. What is the primary key for the Customers table?
    • CustomerID
  3. How are the Orders table and the OrderDetails table related?

    They have a one-to-many relationship linked by the OrderID field. OrderID acts as the Primary Key in the Orders table and as a Foreign Key in the OrderDetails table.

This lesson emphasizes the importance of hands-on practice activities that utilize schema and data from the well-known Northwind to effectively teach query concepts before the introduction of SQL. It includes a comprehensive summary that clarifies relational models, entities, and interrelationships, thereby laying a solid foundation for understanding database structures. Furthermore, the lesson is supplemented with a glossary of essential terminology, including key concepts such as attributes, primary keys, and indexes, which are crucial for grasping the fundamentals of database management. Schemas play a critical role in database management by providing a blueprint for how data is structured within a database.
